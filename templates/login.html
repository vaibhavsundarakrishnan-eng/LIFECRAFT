ABOUT LIFECRAFT

LifeCraft is an interactive career and financial life-simulation platform designed to help users explore how their decisions can shape their future.

The platform combines a career recommendation system with a financial simulator. Based on a user's interests, strengths, and preferences, LifeCraft recommends suitable career paths and provides insights into why they may be a good fit.

Users can then experience the financial side of their choices by managing simulated income, expenses, savings, investments, and unexpected life events.

Rather than simply predicting a future, LifeCraft allows users to explore different possibilities and see the consequences of their decisions.
