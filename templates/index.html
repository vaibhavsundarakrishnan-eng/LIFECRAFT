ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is LIFECRAFT?

LIFECRAFT is an interactive decision-support application designed to help students explore career possibilities and understand financial choices through simulation.

The system uses a questionnaire-based career compatibility engine to analyse academic interests, personal interests and work preferences. It then provides career paths for exploration, including information about courses, skills and other career-related factors.

LIFECRAFT also includes a separate Financial Simulator that allows users to explore how their financial situation could change over a selected period of up to five years.

LIFECRAFT is an educational simulation intended for exploration and learning. Its career compatibility results and financial projections should not be treated as professional career or financial advice.
